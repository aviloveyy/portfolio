Final reflection

From completing tasks to communicating with purpose

The early activities showed me that meaning is shaped by culture, experience and community. Working on symbols, gestures and values taught me to question assumptions, which matters because professionals work with people who do not share their experiences.

Business writing added another responsibility: helping readers decide what to do next. Defining effective writing and analyzing a feedback email taught me to value clarity, courtesy, specificity and action, and to see revision as a normal part of quality work.

My inflation talk brought these ideas into speaking. Feedback on storytelling, audience questions and lighter slides tells me what to do next: build presentations people remember for the ideas, not the amount of text.
